Mageywallet privacy policy

Effective 23 November 2026 · Applies to the Mageywallet app for Android (com.salhi.mageywallet)

In short

  • Everything you put into Mageywallet stays on your phone.
  • The app has no internet access, so it cannot send anything anywhere. Android enforces this.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics.
  • We, the developer, never receive any of your data.

Who we are

Mageywallet is a personal spending tracker for the Maldives, made by Hussain Shah Jaufar. You can contact us at dhiyaacode@gmail.com.

What the app keeps on your phone

To work, Mageywallet saves the following in its own storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it.

  • Your entries: amounts, dates, categories, notes and which account they belong to.
  • Your accounts: names you give them, the bank, and the account number if you add it.
  • Bank slips the app has read: the details printed on the slip (bank, amount, date, reference, the sender's and receiver's names and account numbers, remarks), and a link to the image on your phone. The image itself is not copied.
  • Shops and people: names and account numbers seen on your slips, with the category you chose for them, so their next slips are sorted for you.
  • Money you lent or borrowed: the person's name, the amounts, the pay-back date and, if you add it, their phone number.
  • Your profile and settings: the name shown in the greeting and your choices in Settings.

Photos

If you allow it, Mageywallet reads images in the photo albums where bank slips are usually saved (for example WhatsApp, Screenshots and Downloads, or the albums you choose) to find bank transfer and bill payment slips. You can also pick photos yourself, or share a slip into the app from another app.

The text on each image is read on your phone by Google's ML Kit text recognition, which is built into the app. Images are never uploaded, copied or changed. Images that are not slips are skipped; the app keeps only a link to each one and a note that it was checked, so it is not read again.

No internet access

Mageywallet does not have Android's internet permission, so Android does not let it connect to the internet at all. The ML Kit library normally sends Google usage figures about how it performs. In Mageywallet that is switched off and cannot happen, because the app has no network connection.

Notifications and reminders

If you allow notifications, Mageywallet shows reminders on your phone on the day someone said they would pay you back, or the day you said you would pay someone. These are made on the phone; nothing is sent.

When you tap Remind, Call, SMS or WhatsApp for a person, Mageywallet opens that app with a short message or number filled in. You see the message and choose whether to send it. What happens in that app is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

Backup

Mageywallet's data is excluded from Google's cloud backup. On Android 12 and newer, if you choose to copy everything from your old phone to a new one during set-up, Android may copy Mageywallet's data straight from one phone to the other.

Because nothing is backed up online, losing or resetting your phone also loses your Mageywallet data.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone, and we cannot, because it never leaves your phone.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete entries, accounts, people and slips in the app. Uninstalling Mageywallet, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes everything it saved.

Security

Your data is kept in Mageywallet's private storage, which Android keeps separate from other apps. Anyone who can unlock your phone and open the app can see it. You can hide amounts on screen from the eye icon on Home.

Children

Mageywallet is meant for adults managing their own money and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If a future version of the app ever needs to send data off your phone, this policy will say so before that version is released.
