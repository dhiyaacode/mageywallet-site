Support

Help with Mageywallet for Android.

Contact

Questions, problems or ideas:

dhiyaacode@gmail.com

To help sort out a problem quickly, please include:

  • your phone model and Android version;
  • the Mageywallet version, shown at the bottom of Settings;
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.

If a slip was read wrongly, say which bank it was from and what was wrong. If you attach the slip, cover the names, account numbers and reference first. Never send your banking password or a code from your bank.

Common questions

Which banks does Mageywallet read?

Transfer slips and bill payment slips from BML (Bank of Maldives) and MIB (Maldives Islamic Bank). Statements can be checked from BML, as CSV or Excel files. Other banks may follow.

Does Mageywallet connect to my bank?

No. It never logs in to your bank and cannot make payments. It only reads the slips you already have on your phone. Mageywallet is not made by, or affiliated with, any bank.

My slips are not being found
  • Allow Mageywallet to see your photos when it asks. You can change this later in Android's settings under Apps › Mageywallet › Permissions.
  • In Find bank slips, open Where to look and make sure the album with your slips is chosen (for example WhatsApp, Viber, Screenshots or Downloads).
  • Check how far back it is looking; older slips need a longer period.
  • You can also pick a slip yourself, or share it into Mageywallet from WhatsApp, Viber or your bank app.
A slip was sorted the wrong way

Tap the slip before adding it to change whether it is spending, income or a move between your own accounts, and its category. Mageywallet uses your name and account numbers to tell which side is you, so check that your accounts have their account numbers filled in.

Pay-back reminders do not appear
  • Allow notifications for Mageywallet in Android's settings.
  • Some phones stop apps in the background to save battery. If reminders are missed, allow Mageywallet to run in the background, or turn off battery optimisation for it. Where this setting lives depends on the phone make.
Is my data backed up?

No. Mageywallet has no internet access, and its data is left out of Google's cloud backup, so nothing is stored online. If you lose or reset your phone, your Mageywallet data is lost too.

On Android 12 and newer, if you copy everything from your old phone to a new one during set-up, Android may copy Mageywallet's data straight across.

How do I hide my amounts?

Tap the eye icon on Home to hide amounts on screen. Tap it again to show them.

How do I change the language or theme?

Open Settings and choose Language (English or Dhivehi) and Theme (Day, Night or Same as phone).

How do I delete my data?

You can delete entries, accounts, people and slips inside the app. To delete everything, uninstall Mageywallet or clear its storage in Android's settings. The developer has no copy, so there is nothing to ask us to delete.

Does it cost anything?

No. Mageywallet is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
