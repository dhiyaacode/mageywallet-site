Made for how we pay here

Most payments in the Maldives are bank transfers, and every one leaves a slip. Mageywallet turns those slips into a clear record.

Reads your bank slips

  • Finds BML and MIB transfer and bill payment slips saved in your photos.
  • Or share a slip straight in from WhatsApp, Viber or your bank app.
  • Reads the amount, date, reference, sender and receiver on the phone, and spots the same slip saved twice.

Sorts itself

  • Works out income, spending or a move between your own accounts.
  • Pick a category for a shop or person once; their next slips are sorted for you.
  • Nothing is added until you check it.

Bank and cash balances

  • Bank accounts and cash in one place.
  • Taking cash out or moving money between your accounts is not counted as spending.
  • MVR and USD are kept apart and never added together.

See the month

  • What came in, what went out and what is left, on Home.
  • Categories with their own kinds, such as Food › Takeaway.
  • Reports by month and by category.

Money between friends

  • Keep track of money you lent and money you borrowed.
  • A reminder on your phone on the pay-back date.
  • Send a friendly reminder in English or Dhivehi. You see the message and send it yourself.

Check with your statement

  • Open a BML statement (CSV or Excel) to see what is missing.
  • Lines already added from a slip are recognised, so nothing is counted twice.

A look inside

Tap a screen to see it larger. Screens use made-up names and amounts.

Private by design

Your money is your business. Mageywallet is built so it cannot share it.

No internet access

The app does not have Android's internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

Read on your phone

Slips are read on the phone. Images are never uploaded, copied or changed.

No account, no ads

No sign-in, no advertising and no tracking. The developer never receives your data.

Hide amounts

One tap hides amounts on screen when someone is looking.
